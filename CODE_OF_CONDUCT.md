# Code of Conduct

Be direct, respectful, and technical. Harassment, personal attacks, and abusive
behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers privately. Include the project space
where the behavior occurred, relevant links or screenshots, and whether there is
an immediate safety or moderation concern.

Maintainers may remove abusive content, limit participation, or take other
reasonable moderation steps to keep project spaces focused and safe.
