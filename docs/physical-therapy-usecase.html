The Gap Between Visits

You see your physical therapist for 45 minutes, twice a week. The other 167 hours? You're on your own. A printed handout is the only bridge between your clinic visit and your couch. That gap is where recovery stalls — and where most patients quietly give up.

Exercises Forgotten

You leave the clinic confident, but by day three the handout is buried under mail. Was it 3 sets of 12, or 2 sets of 15? Only 35% of patients actually complete their home exercises.

No One to Ask

"Is this pain normal?" "Should I push through or stop?" Between visits, patients have questions and no one to answer them. They either overdo it, or they stop entirely.

Generic Programs

Paper handouts can't adapt. If clamshells are too easy, the program doesn't progress. If bridges cause pain, the handout doesn't know. One-size-fits-all means optimal for nobody.

Recovery Stalls Out

Without daily accountability, motivation fades. 70% of patients drop out of PT before their care plan is complete. Longer recovery, higher re-injury rates, worse outcomes.

The Solution: An AI Recovery Team

Instead of a printed handout and a "see you Thursday," your clinic deploys a squad of AI agents that stay with the patient between visits. Each agent has a specific role in the patient's recovery — delivering exercises, tracking progress, answering questions, and keeping the care plan on track.

AI recovery team: four specialist agents supporting the patient between clinic visits

All four agents share the same context about the patient — treatment history, prescribed exercises, pain trends, appointment schedule. The Exercise Coach knows which exercises were prescribed. The Recovery Tracker knows yesterday's pain score. The Education Agent knows the diagnosis and can explain what's happening in plain language. They work together so the patient gets consistent, informed support — not four disconnected bots.

How It Works

The patient's experience is simple: they get messages on WhatsApp or SMS from their clinic. Behind the scenes, each clinic's agents run in an isolated, secure environment on OpenClaw Machines — with full context about every patient's recovery.

Clinician prescribes, agents deliver and track, patient responds via text — all in a secure, isolated environment

Why This Works for Patients

  • No app to download — Everything happens over SMS or WhatsApp. The patient texts back like they would with anyone else. Zero onboarding friction.
  • The agent remembers everything — Pain scores, which exercises were hard, what modifications were made. Persistent memory means the agent gets smarter about each patient over time.
  • Adapts to the patient — Morning person? Check-ins at 8 AM. Night owl? 7 PM. Clamshells too easy? Agent suggests the clinician progress them. The experience isn't static like a handout.
  • Clinician stays in the loop — The agent feeds compliance data and pain trends to the clinician before the next visit. No time wasted on "so, did you do your exercises?"

Key difference from generic AI tools: ChatGPT can describe how to do a bridge. It can't deliver your specific exercise program on a schedule, check in the next morning, track your pain over time, adjust to your response patterns, and brief your PT before Thursday's session. An agent squad can.

The Patient Journey

Personalized Exercise Programs

Right after the session, the patient gets their exercise program on their phone — not a printout they'll lose. Each exercise includes the name, sets, reps, specific cues from their clinician, and a link to a demonstration video. The program lives in the conversation thread, always findable.

  1. Clinician prescribes exercises at end of session
  2. Patient receives program via WhatsApp/SMS within minutes
  3. Each exercise: name, sets/reps, form cues, video link
  4. Patient can scroll back to review anytime — no lost handouts
  5. Program updates automatically when clinician adjusts the plan

Daily Check-Ins & Pain Tracking

Every day, the agent checks in — at a time that fits the patient's schedule. "Did you get your exercises done?" A simple yes/no. Then a pain score. If the patient reports increased pain, the agent gives practical guidance and flags it for the clinician. Over days and weeks, this builds a recovery picture that no twice-weekly visit could capture.

  1. Agent messages at the patient's preferred time
  2. Quick check: exercise completion + pain score (0–10)
  3. Agent responds with encouragement, or practical advice if pain increased
  4. Tracks trends over time: "Pain down from 5 to 3 this week"
  5. Alerts clinician if pain spikes or patient misses multiple days

Appointment Reminders & Recovery Milestones

No more voicemails from the front desk. The agent sends appointment reminders via text, lets patients reschedule with a reply, and celebrates recovery milestones along the way — "You've completed 3 weeks of exercises and your pain is down 40% since you started."

  1. Reminder 24 hours before appointment, with easy reschedule option
  2. Patient replies "yes" to confirm or "reschedule" to change
  3. Milestone messages when pain scores improve or streaks are hit
  4. Gentle re-engagement if the patient goes quiet for a few days
  5. Reduces no-show rates by making the appointment feel expected, not forgotten

Security & Compliance

Healthcare data demands more than "we encrypt everything." The OpenClaw Machines architecture provides defense-in-depth through hardware isolation, layered authentication, and complete audit trails — all the foundations required for HIPAA-aligned deployments.

KVM Hardware Isolation

Each clinic runs in its own Firecracker MicroVM with KVM-level separation — not shared containers or namespace boundaries. A compromised agent cannot access another clinic's memory space.

4-Layer Authentication

Every request passes through JWT validation, proxy tokens, agent tokens, and machine-scoped tokens. Each layer validates a different trust boundary. No single token compromise grants full access.

Encrypted Secrets

API keys, messaging credentials, and patient tokens are stored in GCP Secret Manager. They're injected at runtime, never written to disk, and scoped to individual clinic environments.

Complete Audit Trail

Every agent action — every portal login, every form submission, every patient message — is logged with timestamps, agent identity, and action details. Auditable by default.

Network Isolation

MicroVMs sit on isolated bridge networks. VM-to-VM traffic is blocked by iptables. All external traffic routes through Cloudflare tunnels — zero public IPs exposed.

Data Residency Control

Choose the GCP region where your clinic's MicroVM runs. Patient data stays in the region you select. No cross-region replication without explicit configuration.
